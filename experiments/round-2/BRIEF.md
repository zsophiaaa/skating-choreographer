# Brief — round 2

Same as `experiments/BRIEF.md` (read it and every skill first), with two
changes:

1. **Design your own program. Do not copy the order of `lalaland-c3` or of any
   round-1 version.** Everything the skater has *said* still holds (their
   profile in `skater-profile`: the content, the two stops and the hop, one of
   each highlight, the tail after the hop, crossovers as the connecting
   material, dancing hands), but where the jumps go on the ice, which recipe
   carries each one, how the front is built, and how the step sequence is
   laid out are yours. Round 1 produced five variations of one skeleton; this
   round is for finding out whether something *different* can also be right.
2. **Count your steers.** In `notes.md` say how many `steer` runs you needed,
   what each one gave (ends, sequence span, rotation), and which lever fixed
   it. The skills now claim the levers are documented — test that claim.

Deliverables as before, in your own folder: `program.json`, `notes.md`,
`verify.json`. Keep every intermediate file in your folder.
